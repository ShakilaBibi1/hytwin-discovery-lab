What did the research find?

Explore the reported associations between sex, ethnicity and HyperTrajectory membership in UK Biobank.

The bigger picture: sex matters

Across ethnic groups in both datasets, males had higher HyperScores than females. Ethnicity-related trajectory differences were more evident among females. Overall ethnic differences in UK Biobank HyperScore were not statistically significant (p = 0.056).

These percentages express relative odds of trajectory membership versus all other trajectories, not an individual's probability of damage. Unpublished manuscript results require approval before public promotion.
